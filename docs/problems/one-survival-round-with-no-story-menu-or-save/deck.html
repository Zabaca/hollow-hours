How it plays

Between missions the city stays open and hunted; each finished mission is saved

  1. Free roam. The next mission's start glows on the radar, the map and in the street.
  2. Start. Drive into it: a radio call, a title card, the first objective.
  3. Steps. Reach, hold still, run down, stay alive, beat a timer; calls play as you drive.
  4. Checkpoint. After each pickup. A failure offers Retry from checkpoint, Restart or Quit.
  5. Done. "MISSION COMPLETE", a closing call naming the next start, an automatic save.

Recommendation

Recommended: option 1, because a story-wide clock works against the free roam you asked for

  • Free roam stays free. With a clock, every minute of roaming is a minute less night, so players stop roaming.
  • Failures do not stack. A hard mission retried a few times cannot leave too little night for the next.
  • Dawn still means the end: first light comes as you drive out through the gate.
  • The dread stays where it counts: the stops, the shutter timer, the last minute before the gate.
