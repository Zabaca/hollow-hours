On the drive

Long drives put the danger where the car has to slow down

  • Roadblocks and squeezes. Wrecks, a jackknifed truck and barriers cut the straight routes: every leg forces a turn or a squeeze down to about 30 km/h.
  • Ambushes. Two or three Gaunts wait beside the squeezes and bridge ends, out of the headlights, and come from the side.
  • The pack follows you. New Gaunts arrive ahead of the car; ones left far behind drop out.
  • A route on the radar and the map, and a radar that sees further at speed.

Why there

Today a fast car on a straight is near-harmless: creatures ahead are run down 83% of the time, and a Gaunt tops out at 63 to 74 km/h. The danger sits at corners, stops and crashes, so the city makes more of those.
