## Solution

### How big

The city grows by rings of blocks round today's quarter, on the same street grid (roads every 64 m east to west and 48 m north to south, 14 m wide, as today). The number of rings is one setting, so the city can grow later without a rebuild of the method.

| Rings round today's quarter | City (wall to wall) | Blocks | Area | Mission legs by road | At 60 km/h | At 90 km/h |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none (today) | 140 by 108 m | 4 | 1x | 18 to 131 m (straight line) | 1 to 8 s | under 6 s |
| **2 (recommended)** | **about 396 by 300 m** | **36** | **about 8x** | **about 310 to 560 m** | **19 to 34 s** | **12 to 22 s** |
| 3 | about 524 by 396 m | 64 | about 14x | about 410 to 740 m | 25 to 44 s | 16 to 30 s |
| 4 | about 652 by 492 m | 100 | about 21x | about 510 to 920 m | 31 to 55 s | 20 to 37 s |

The leg lengths are estimates along the street grid between the places sketched below, before any roadblock; the mission test measures the real ones. In play, corners, a roadblock's detour and the pack add time, so a 2-ring leg is aimed at 25 to 40 seconds as played (an inference, to be checked in the owner's hand test). The lead's two suggestions do not meet: a city two to three times wider gives legs of about 20 to 35 seconds; 30 to 60 seconds needs about five times. Two rings are recommended for this round because it already makes every leg three to twenty times longer than today, it is 36 blocks to build and keep fast instead of 64 or 100, and a third ring is a later change of one setting plus moving the landmarks out (about 2 to 3 agent hours).

### What stays and what moves

- **Today's quarter stays where it is, block for block**: the pharmacy tower, diner, motel, bar, pawn shop, the towers, the burned bus, the lamps and wrecks, at the same map positions. Its wall comes down (the city's wall is now the outer one) and its outer streets carry on into the new districts. Three things in it change, so each landmark exists once: the old hotel tower's sign goes dark and the tower is just a tower; the old gas station's 24H sign and lights go out (a dead station); the ranger's hut leaves the old park (its trees, fountain and benches stay).
- **The pharmacy stays**: north side of the avenue, as Teo's line says, with the run-down scene that was tuned there (the two Gaunts coming up the avenue from the west).

### The districts

Each new block is filled from a kit of block types, chosen and varied from a seed so no two look alike, in today's look (the same materials, windows, signs, awnings, lamps and wrecks):

- **West, the cordon side**: warehouses with loading doors, fenced yards with containers, a rail siding, and the canal.
- **North**: brick rows of three to five storeys with shopfronts and awnings, the new 24-hour gas station and shop in the far corner.
- **East**: towers with lit windows here and there, the new hotel tower with its fire escape and east door in the far south-east.
- **South**: low houses with yards and dead trees, a school or church hall, empty lots with burning barrels.
- **The big park** in the north-east (two blocks merged) with the ranger's hut at its gate.
- **The canal** runs north to south through the west districts in place of one street, with three bridges, one of them on the avenue (so the gate is still at the avenue's end). The routes to the shop, from the shop and to the gate cross it.

Shop signs use plain words that are ours (LAUNDRY, HARDWARE, NOODLES, BAKERY, CINEMA and the like), each landmark's word used once. Everything is original; nothing is taken from any other game, film or real city.

### What happens on a longer drive

A longer drive on long straights would be a longer quiet stretch: at 60 km/h on a straight the pack is near-harmless, and its danger sits at corners, stops and crashes (the encounters review). So the danger is put where the car has to slow down:

1. **Roadblocks and squeezes.** Wreck barricades, a truck jackknifed across a street and police barriers cut the straight routes. Each mission leg's shortest way crosses at least one: either it forces a turn into a side street, or it leaves a gap about a car and a half wide that takes the car down to about 30 km/h. The burned bus stays across the central junction.
2. **Ambushes at the squeezes and bridges.** Two or three Gaunts wait in the dark beside a roadblock, a squeeze or a bridge end, out of the headlights, and come at the car from the side as it slows within about 35 m. Each mission leg has one or two; in free roam a few spots are armed at random, so a known route is not a safe one.
3. **The pack follows the car.** New Gaunts come in 60 to 120 m from the car, ahead of it and toward where it is going, never in its headlights; one left more than 150 m behind that is not hunting is quietly dropped and replaced ahead. So the city is as hunted round the car as today's quarter is, wherever the car is. Survive the Night gets the same.
4. **Dark stretches and lit refuges.** About one lamp in five lives, as today, gathered at a few junctions and the gas station's canopy, so a driver can see where to breathe; the long avenues between are dark.

The pack's own numbers (its size, the kill speeds, the pounce) are unchanged; they are tuned from the owner's hand play.

### Finding the way

- **A route on the radar and the full map**: a faint amber line along the streets to the objective, round the roadblocks, redrawn twice a second.
- **The radar widens with speed**: about 60 m to its rim when crawling, about 110 m flat out, so a fast car sees the next turn coming.
- **The full map** shows the whole city north-up with the places, the canal and its bridges, and the roadblocks.
- **During West Gate's minute** the gate is on the radar and the map (today nothing is marked while you hold out), and the objective reads "Get to the west gate. It opens in a minute." (text only, nothing voiced).

### The radio

No line is re-recorded and nothing is bought. The 12 lines that name a place or a time stay true: the start is on the avenue; the hotel has Vera on its roof and an east door; the all-night shop is at the gas station with a south door; the next caller is near the park hut; the pharmacy is a tower on the north side of the avenue; the gas station has the aerial; the west gate is at the end of the avenue; the gate opens in sixty seconds and stays open twenty. "The quarter" now names the whole walled area; the skyline beyond the wall is the rest of the city.

### The detail pass (the last slice, optional)

Folded in here rather than done on today's four blocks first, because its materials and props belong to the block kit: done once, it lands on all 36 blocks, today's four included. Done first on today's quarter it would have to be redone for 32 new blocks, and judged on a city about to change. It is the last slice so the owner plays the bigger city plain first, and it waits on his yes. What it adds: tiling textures for brick, concrete, plaster, asphalt and pavement (made in Blender from its own procedural textures, as the creature's skin was, so free and ours), bevelled building edges, window frames and sills, and more props (roof units, drainpipes, phone poles with sagging wires, bins and bags, bus stops, chain-link fences, cones).

## Trade-offs

- **Keeping today's quarter rather than starting fresh.** The owner's known streets, the tuned pharmacy scene, and the tests that drive on the avenue's west stretch all keep working. The cost: the centre is the plainest, hand-made part of the city, three of its landmarks lose their role, and its street grid sets the grid for everything.
- **A grid rather than an irregular street plan** (option 03). The simulation's walls stay square to the map, as the car's collisions (ported from Roadkill and checked against it) need, and the time roughly halves. The city reads as a grid; the canal, the merged park, the yards and the roadblocks break it up.
- **A kit of block types rather than today's four blocks repeated** (option 02). About 2 to 3 more agent hours for a city that does not repeat itself.
- **Two rings rather than three or four.** Legs of 19 to 34 seconds at 60 km/h, short of the 30 to 60 suggested; in exchange, half or a third of the blocks, and less risk to the frame rate and download. Growing it later is a setting.
- **Moving the landmarks rather than re-recording lines.** No money, and the story reads the same; the old hotel tower, gas station and park are quieter than before.
- **Danger at the squeezes rather than retuning the pack.** The pack's tested behaviour stays as it is; the city does the work. If straights still feel safe in the hand play, the mid-leap kill speed is the lever (docs/follow-ups.md).
- **A route line on the radar.** It helps in a city 8 times the size and is what open-world driving games do; it also makes getting lost less likely, which some would call part of the dread.

## Live Effects

Nothing. The game is not live: only the owner plays it. Nothing is bought: no voice is re-recorded and no image or texture is paid for. As with every change here, what is merged is public, because the repository is.

## User Stories

1. As a player, I want a mission drive to take tens of seconds, so that a mission feels like a drive across a city and not a string of stops.
2. As a player, I want the city to look varied, so that I can tell where I am without the map.
3. As a player, I want today's streets still at the centre, so that the city I learned is still there.
4. As a player, I want each landmark to exist once, so that "the hotel" and "the gas station" are never in doubt.
5. As a player, I want the radio's directions to stay true, so that the story still makes sense.
6. As a player, I want roadblocks that make me choose a way round or squeeze through slowly, so that a route is a decision.
7. As a player, I want Gaunts to come at me from the side where I have to slow down, so that a long drive has danger in it.
8. As a player, I want the pack to find me wherever I am, so that no part of the city is safe.
9. As a player, I want a few lit places on the way, so that I know where I can catch my breath.
10. As a player, I want a route on the radar and the map, so that I can drive fast without getting lost.
11. As a player, I want the radar to show further when I am going fast, so that I see the next turn in time.
12. As a player, I want the west gate marked while I wait for it to open, so that I can be there when it does.
13. As a player, I want the canal's bridges to matter, so that the city has places I remember.
14. As a player of Survive the Night, I want the round to stay as dangerous as it is today, so that the bigger city does not make it easy.
15. As a player with a save, I want Continue to carry on in the bigger city, so that I do not lose my progress.
16. As a player on a modest computer, I want the bigger city to run as smoothly as today's, so that it stays playable.
17. As the owner, I want no voice spend for this, so that the city is bigger at no cost.
18. As the owner, I want to play the bigger city plain before the detail pass, so that I judge the drives before the looks.
19. As a builder, I want the size to be one setting, so that the city can grow later.
20. As a builder, I want a test that measures every leg by road, so that a change to the city cannot shrink the drives unnoticed.
21. As a builder, I want the simulation to cost no more per step in the big city than in today's, so that the tests and the game stay fast.

## Implementation Decisions

- **The city script keeps its shape and its look.** Today's materials, building, lamp, tree, wreck, bus, barrier, dumpster, barrel and hydrant helpers stay, and today's four block functions stay at their coordinates, changed only as "What stays and what moves" says. New: a block kit (one function per block type, filling a given block rectangle from a seed of its own, so a change to one block never moves another), landmark functions for the new gas station, hotel, park and hut, a canal with bridges, roadblocks, and a ring count that sets the arena. The perimeter wall, the gate gap and frame, the dark ground and the skyline ring are built from the arena, so they move out with it.
- **One model piece per block.** Each block, with its pavement and its stretch of road, is its own piece in the exported city, so the renderer can skip what is out of view. Small props get a visibility range; the camera's far plane comes in to where the fog has hidden everything (measured in a screenshot), with the skyline inside it; buildings are occluders.
- **The layout gains** the roads as a list (centre lines, widths, where the canal replaces one), the roadblocks, the canal and its bridges, and the ambush spots, beside today's records. The gate record moves with the wall.
- **The world answers from buckets.** The simulation's world sorts its boxes, posts and lights into cells of about 16 m; every question (the car's collisions, pushing a creature out of a wall, line of sight, is a point lit, how far a point is from a wall) looks only at the cells it touches. The answers stay the same as today's full loops.
- **The path grid** marks each box's and post's own cells instead of testing every cell against every box, giving the same solid cells.
- **The camera's wall test** asks the world's buckets instead of copying and looping every box each frame.
- **The pack's director** places new Gaunts 60 to 120 m from the car (today: any hiding place 60 m or more away), preferring those ahead of the car and toward its objective, never lit or in the headlights; one that is not hunting or holding on and is more than 150 m behind the car is removed and replaced ahead. A story's placed hunters and alerts work as today.
- **Ambushes are story data.** An ambush spot is a place and a count. A step's effects can arm one, and a step's timed effects gain a trigger by distance to a place as well as by seconds. When the car comes within about 35 m of an armed spot, its Gaunts are placed out of the headlights in the side street and begin hunting. In free roam a few spots are armed at random from the layout's list, re-armed after a while.
- **The story's places** move as the table says, and the survive step of West Gate names the gate as its place so it is marked. The safe start moves; Survive the Night's start stays the layout's own.
- **The autopilot** reads the arena from the world, does its run-down passes along the avenue either side of the pharmacy's place (not fixed numbers; this closes the follow-up), and plans its routes on its own path grid across the whole city.
- **The map** draws roads, the canal, bridges and roadblocks from the layout and the gate from its record; the radar's range follows the car's speed; a route from the car's path grid to the objective is drawn on the radar and the map.
- **The detail pass**, if taken: textures baked from Blender's procedural textures into small tiling images, bevels and frames in the building helper, props in the kit; the same material names, so the game's wet ground and glass restyling still applies.

## Testing Decisions

- **The seam stays the one the tests use today**: step the game simulation in story or survival mode and read its events, plus the layout data for checks on places. No new seam.
- **The bucket world gives the same answers as the full loops**: on today's city, for a sweep of points and lines, collisions, pushes, line of sight and lit points match exactly.
- **The path grid** built the new way has the same solid cells as the old way on today's city.
- **A step costs no more in a big city**: a test layout made by repeating today's layout nine times, stepped with a full pack, takes no more time per step than today's city does (recorded before and after).
- **The pack follows**: after a 60-second drive across the big city, the live pack is within 150 m of the car, and new Gaunts appear 60 to 120 m away, out of the light.
- **Every leg by road**: for each mission leg and the two free-roam legs, the road distance on the car's path grid is at least 300 m, and the escape at least 450 m; every place is inside the arena, clear of everything with the car's clearance, and reachable from the safe start (today's check, kept).
- **Each mission leg crosses a roadblock or a bridge** on its shortest route.
- **Ambushes**: an armed spot places its Gaunts when the car comes within range and not before, out of the headlights; a spot not armed does nothing.
- **The whole story, driven**: the autopilot plays all three missions in the bigger city with the pack off within a raised time bound, and the live-hunter guard runs as today.
- **Unchanged**: the car test, the pack test, the settings, menu, presenter, radio and story-input tests pass as they are; their placements on today's avenue and by the bus still hold, because today's quarter keeps its coordinates. A test whose placement relied on today's outer wall is moved, and says why.
- **Frame time and download size**: from slice 2 on, the builder logs frame time and draw calls on one fixed autopilot drive, High and Low, before and after, and the Mac build's size; they are reported, not asserted.
- **Screenshots** (the builder's own): each district, a roadblock squeeze, a bridge, the gate in the new wall, the full map, the radar with the route; after slice 4, the same frames again.

## Further Notes

### Assumptions (each can be overturned)

1. **60 km/h is the planning speed**, the lead's measure; leg times at 90 km/h are given beside it. Neither is measured from play.
2. **Road distance is estimated along the grid** between the sketched places, before roadblocks; the test is the arbiter.
3. **"The quarter" can name the whole walled city**, since the lines never say how big it is.
4. **The old hotel, gas station and hut go dark or go**, rather than staying as second copies, so each landmark the radio names exists once.
5. **The pack's size stays as the story sets it**, three to six; the director keeping it near the car is enough to keep the city hunted.
6. **Frame time stays near today's** if the renderer skips what the fog hides; this is an inference until slice 2's measurement, and nobody has measured a weak graphics card yet.
7. **The city model grows roughly with its area**, from 2.2 MB to around 15 MB before compression, which would put the Mac build somewhere near 75 to 80 MB (an inference; repeated props can be instanced if it grows past that).
8. **The canal is a wall to the car and the creatures** except at its bridges; the simulation has no water or height.
