The hotel, the 24-hour gas station and the park hut would have moved to the far corners, with the pharmacy staying, so every leg crossed the bigger city without changing a radio line, and the west gate would have moved to the new outer wall. The pieces that do not depend on growing the quarter carry over into option 04: the block kit, the simulation's work for a big city, the danger at roadblocks and squeezes, the pack following the car, the route on the radar, and the detail pass.
