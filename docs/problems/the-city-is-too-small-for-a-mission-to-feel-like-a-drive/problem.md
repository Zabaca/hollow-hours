## What exists today (read in the code, commit 0198563)

- **The city is hand-made, one block at a time.** A headless Blender script builds four blocks between three east-west streets (y = -48, 0, 48; the middle one is the avenue) and three north-south ones (x = -64, 0, 64), 14 m wide, blocks 50 by 34 m. Each block is its own function placing its buildings, props, lights and creature hiding places by hand: the pharmacy tower, diner and motel (north-west); the 24-hour gas station and shop, a parking lot and a warehouse (north-east); the dead park and the ranger's hut (south-west); the bar, pawn shop and hotel towers (south-east). A 4.5 m concrete wall runs round the arena (x from -70 to 70, y from -54 to 54) with the gate's gap at the avenue's west end, and a ring of dark towers stands beyond it as a skyline. The script also writes the layout the game reads: 46 boxes, 54 posts, 13 lights, 17 wrecks and 24 hiding places today. The exported city model is 2.2 MB.
- **The legs today** (straight-line, from the story's places): park hut to pharmacy 18 m, shop to hotel 27 m, start to shop 77 m, pharmacy to hotel 106 m, hotel to west gate 131 m. At 60 km/h that is 1 to 8 seconds; the car's top speed on the flat is about 110 km/h.
- **The simulation looks at the whole city on every question.** Every collision, every line-of-sight test, every "is this creature lit" and every push of a creature out of a wall loops over all the boxes, posts or lights, many times a step at 120 steps a second. The creatures' path grid is built by testing every 2 m cell against every box and post. Both are fine for four blocks; their cost grows with the city's size (inferred, not measured).
- **The pack does not follow the car.** A new Gaunt appears at any dark hiding place at least 60 m from the car and out of its headlights. In a bigger city most hiding places are hundreds of metres away, so a pack of three to six would thin out over the larger area (inferred from the code).
- **Danger sits at corners, stops and crashes.** The encounters review found that at 60 km/h on a straight the pack is near-harmless (creatures ahead are run down 83% of the time), and that hunters lead a fast car and end up ahead of it. A longer drive on long straights would be a longer quiet stretch unless something slows the car or comes from the side.
- **Things that assume today's city:** the map's roads are three fixed lines each way and its gate gap is drawn at a fixed spot; the autopilot's run-down tactic uses fixed avenue coordinates, and its "room to boost" check uses today's arena edges; about 40 placements in the tests put the car on the avenue's west stretch or by the burned bus; the camera's wall test copies and loops every box each frame; the camera draws up to 600 m away, though the fog hides almost everything past about 95 m.
- **The recorded radio lines pin places, not distances.** 12 of the 21 lines name a place or a time, and each states a relation a bigger city can keep: the car starts on the avenue; Vera is on the hotel roof and takes deliveries at its east door; the all-night shop is at the gas station and is loaded at its south door; the next voice is near the park hut; the pharmacy is a tower on the north side of the avenue; the gas station has the aerial; the west gate is at the end of the avenue; the gate opens sixty seconds after the pickup and stays open twenty. None names a distance or a street.
- **Saves hold mission names only**, never positions, so a save made in today's city continues in any other.
- **Survive the Night** (the old five-minute round) plays in the same city from the layout's own start on the avenue.
- **A stopgap is being built now**, on its own branch: the checkpoints re-placed inside today's city so each leg is at least 90 m. It is out of this problem's scope; whatever it places is replaced by the bigger city's places.
- **The "city detail pass"** (textures, bevelled edges, more props; the city reads plain) was offered to the owner and is undecided. It touches the same Blender script and the same frame-time budget as a bigger city.

## Known numbers

- The signed Mac build is 66 MB (the lead's figure; the README's first macOS export was also 66 MB zipped).
- The new creature costs about 6% of frame time on an M3 Ultra (the lead's measure). Frame time on a weak graphics card has never been measured.
